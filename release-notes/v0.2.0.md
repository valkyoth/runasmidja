# Runasmidja 0.2.0 — OpenBao-first secret provisioning

Status: remediation candidate; maintainer pentest RETEST REQUIRED. Original candidate committed locally for pentest,
untagged and not production-ready. Baseline: signed v0.1.0.

The development fixture now starts and initializes verified-TLS OpenBao before
PostgreSQL or Valkey. Three service passwords are issued by OpenBao's random API
and retained in version-1 KV records with create-only writes. Separate scoped
provisioning/runtime identities verify retrieval before consumers start.

Interrupted provisioning and restart reuse the same vault versions and retained
PostgreSQL data. Root revocation is confirmed after scoped provisioning/readiness;
recovery material stays outside service mounts. Sealed/unavailable/denied vaults,
wrong TLS trust and malformed/changed credential records have no local fallback.

Fixture reuse/stop now checks project/service/instance labels on containers,
networks and volumes; container reuse checks the pinned image's local identity.
The new v02-reviewed profile preserves the old fixtures without silently resetting,
importing passwords or rekeying existing data.

Unit regressions cover ordering, create-only issuance, custody, ownership,
version/schema/projection failures and cleanup. Actual service qualification
covers empty-state issuance, partial retry, denials, root-revoked restart,
persistent data and issued-password scans in container metadata/logs and audit.

[local stack](../docs/local-stack.md) and
[handoff](../docs/releases/v0.2.0-handoff.md) for exact evidence and limitations.

fingerprints, temporary delivery, build/release identities, legacy migration,
